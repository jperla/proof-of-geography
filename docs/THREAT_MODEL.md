# Threat Model & Hardening Analysis

## Adversary Capabilities

We assume a strong adversary who:
* Has **full physical and root hypervisor access** to the Prover server, host OS, NIC, BGP routers, and local clocks.
* Can deploy arbitrary low-latency **front-end reverse proxies, VPNs, BGP Anycast nodes, or AI inference routers** in any city worldwide.
* Can falsify **WHOIS, reverse DNS, and RFC 8805 geofeeds**.
* Can route traffic over **private dark fiber, cloud backbones, or Starlink LEO vacuum laser links**.
* Can physically relocate hardware via **truck, cargo ship/barge, or aircraft** after initial provisioning.
* May compromise or collude with **up to $f$ Anchors** (or an entire single cloud provider's regional control plane when committee size $K$ spans $\ge 3$ independent clouds).

We assume standard cryptographic primitives (`Ed25519`, `X25519`, `BLAKE3`, `SHA-512`) and the hardware isolation of the Prover's CPU TEE (AMD SEV-SNP / Intel TDX / AWS Nitro) hold.

## Export-Control & Governance Extensions (Brass & Aarne 2024; Tee & Happel 2026)

Incorporating research insights from **Brass & Aarne (IAPS 2024, *Location Verification for AI Chips*)** and **Tee & Happel (May 2026, arXiv:2605.01930v1, *GPU Fingerprinting for Location Verification*)**, `proof-of-geography` adds eight defense-in-depth capabilities:

1. **Verifier-Enforced Target/Radius & `ChipRegistry`:** `VerificationPolicy` enforces verifier-owned `expected_target_location`, `max_allowed_radius_km`, and `ChipRegistry` (`prover_pk_hex` / `gpu_uuid` → authorized facility).
2. **Negative Anti-Smuggling & Positive Jurisdiction Geofencing:** `pog-geo::geofence` checks both polygon containment and exclusion (including small interior enclaves) and exposes Brass & Aarne's $\min(\|L - R\|) > D$ coverage audit via `pog-cli audit-coverage --restricted-polygon CN`.
3. **"Striking Distance" Anchor Country Filtering:** ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 `country_code` on every Anchor + `excluded_anchor_countries`, `allowed_anchor_countries`, and `min_distinct_countries` in committee selection and offline verification.
4. **Silence / Blocking, Baseline-Shift & Delay-Inflation Detection:** `ContinuityGapDetected`, `BaselineLatencyShiftDetected`, `UniversalDelayInflationSuspected`, and `pog-log` `/v1/anomalies/silent`.
5. **GPU Physical Silicon Fingerprinting (PUF) with Explicit Relay-Headroom Guard:** Seeded `GpuPhysicalFingerprintEvidence` verification (`pog-tee/src/gpu.rs`) that explicitly surfaces why multi-second PUFs (`2.9 s` @ 1% jitter = `4,347 km` vacuum headroom) complement silicon identity without replacing sub-ms UDP timing.
6. **Dark-Fiber / Hollow-Core Fast-Path Detection:** Flags Anchor-pair or Prover receipts beating silica fiber speed (`> 1.02 × 204,000 km/s`) while staying below vacuum $c_0$.
7. **Multi-Chip Cluster Co-Location & Rack-Divergence Audits:** `pog-log` `/v1/clusters` clusters co-located Provers by shared RTT signatures and flags smuggled rack members.
8. **Privacy-Preserving Coarse Jurisdiction Leases (`X-PoG-Lease`):** `pog-proto::lease` (`pog-cli lease issue|verify`) converts a verified private `GeoProofBundle` into a signed coarse jurisdiction operating license exposing **zero** exact city coordinates, raw RTTs, or public IPs—enforcing both wall-clock expiry and an on-chip monotonic countdown (`max_monotonic_lease_seconds`) for region-locking without trusting host NTP.

## Known Limitations & Residual Risks

1. **Physical Side-Channel / Hardware Extraction against a TEE Vendor:** If an attacker extracts the hardware root key from a physical AMD or Intel CPU die via invasive decapping/voltage glitching, they could spoof a single TEE vendor. Mitigations: (a) mix AMD SEV-SNP, Intel TDX, and AWS Nitro across Anchors; (b) physical key extraction on a Prover is costly and detectable via vendor TCB revocations.
2. **Sub-100-Mile Resolution Without a Local Metro Anchor:** Because $1\text{ ms}$ of RTT equals ~93 miles under vacuum speed of light, tightening below ~150 miles requires either a cross-cloud Anchor in the same metro area (e.g., Ashburn, Tokyo, Frankfurt, Singapore, London) or relying on Tier 1B/Tier 2 calibrated bounds over a 24-hour stationary minimum-RTT chain.
3. **Co-located Hardware + Remote Outsourced Compute (Side-Channel Offloading):** A Prover TEE in Zurich could theoretically receive an encrypted prompt, decrypt it, send it over the internet to an unattested GPU in another country, and sign the returned tokens—*unless* the TEE's measured code itself enforces running inference locally inside the enclave / SPDM-bound Confidential GPU (which reproducible open-source TEE images guarantee!).

## Separate Claims: Prover-Only Geography vs. Confidential-GPU Inference Location

Proof of Geography strictly separates two claims so that reverse-proxy geography can **never** be mistaken for proven inference execution location:

1. **`ProverOnlyGeography` (`GeoProofBundle` / `X-PoG-Proof` / `pog-prover --upstream`):**
   - Proves only the physical location of the Prover TEE/proxy that responded to the out-of-band UDP distance-bounding challenge.
   - Responses are tagged `X-PoG-Claim-Mode: prover-only-geography`; `POST /v1/infer` fails closed with `403 Forbidden`.
   - The current `X-PoG-Proof` header alone **never** establishes `ConfidentialGpuInferenceLocation`.
2. **`ConfidentialGpuInferenceLocation` (`ConfidentialInferenceReceipt` / `pog-prover --local-gpu-runtime` / `pog-cli verify-inference`):**
   - Requires a measured local-only runtime digest (`remote_upstream_disabled == true`, `fallback_disabled == true`; combining `--local-gpu-runtime` with `--upstream` fails closed at startup).
   - **PoG proof inside the signed receipt:** The signed per-inference receipt commits to `location_bundle_hash_hex` and `location_epoch_challenge_hex` and embeds the exact independently verifiable `GeoProofBundle` directly in `pog_proof_bundle` (or pairs the signed digest with the exact supplied bundle). The PoG distance-bounding challenge itself stays out-of-band against the multi-cloud Anchors.
   - **End-to-end TLS 1.3 session binding:** Binds the receipt to the **exact** client-to-attested-runtime TLS 1.3 connection via RFC 8446 §7.5 Exported Keying Material (`EXPORTER-pog-inference-v1`, 32 bytes), leaf cert SHA-256 signed by `prover_pk`, and `terminated_in_attested_runtime == true`. Reusing the same TLS certificate on a second TLS handshake or terminating TLS at an intermediate proxy produces a mismatched EKM and fails closed.
   - **Per-request non-interchangeability:** Binds fresh `request_nonce_hex` ($\ge 16$ random bytes), monotonic `tls_connection_request_seq`, monotonic GPU `channel_seq_no`, `request_hash_hex`, `output_hash_hex`, and `gpu_execution_digest_hex`, issued strictly **after** local GPU execution completes. Two requests carried on one keep-alive TLS 1.3 connection cannot swap receipts.
   - **Mutually coupled CPU TEE + Confidential GPU SPDM attestation:** The GPU's ECDSA P-384 signed SPDM quote (chained to a pinned GPU vendor Root CA, `cc_mode_enabled == true`, `debug_disabled == true`) must contain `compute_gpu_binding_nonce(prover_pk, epoch_challenge, spdm_session_transcript_hash)` while the CPU TEE `report_data` commits to `spdm_report_hash_hex` and `spdm_session_transcript_hash_hex`. Two independent valid quotes, a bare quoted GPU ID, or a proxy signature on forwarded output all fail closed.
   - **Honest CPU-GPU Locality Assumptions:** Standard NVIDIA CC SPDM 1.2 encrypted PCIe/NVLink bounce-buffer evidence proves a protected channel and mutual CPU/GPU attestation binding, **not** an independent verifier distance bound across the PCIe/NVLink bus. Verifiers explicitly surface `hardware_locality_established: false` (`SpdmProtectedLocalChannelAssumedColocated`) and `locality_assumptions`, and reject when `require_hardware_rooted_locality: true` unless hardware-rooted PCIe-IDE / NVLink-C2C topology attestation is present.
